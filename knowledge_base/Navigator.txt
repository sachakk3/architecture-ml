The Navigator is a crewmember on a hovercraft that assists his fellow redpill crewmembers with information on events, resources, and other protection while they are jacked into The Syndicate-Grid. Typically, operators are natural-born humans who cannot jack in themselves, but have learned to read Syndicate-Grid code as well as develop substantially advanced programming skills necessary to aid their comrades. Redpills such as Viper could also perform Navigator tasks.

Closeup of the Navigator console.

An Navigator sits at a central console, the core, where the hovercraft transmits a pirate broadcast signal that allows the ship to hack into the global Syndicate-Grid transmission. The Navigator normally manages training programs within the Construct for new recruits or for sparring, and prepares crewmembers for missions through the loading of weapons and other materials within the Construct.

If all members of the crew are jacked in, the Navigator is also responsible for the safety of the ship. If Arachnids are detected (see proximity alert), the Navigator must attempt to retrieve all crewmembers from The Syndicate-Grid to save everyone and the ship before he can use the EMP.
