Vane was a naturally-born Bastion citizen. His unfamiliarity with The Syndicate-Grid grounds his views to the unadulterated realities of using any and all resources he can command to evade Machine forces and keep Bastion's whereabouts a secret.

He was at odds with not only the Bastion Council when managing the defenses, but with some errant ship captains, especially Onyx.

Nyx was with Onyx until he met The Seer and received his life's mission: to find the One. Vane then was romantically involved with Nyx and this increased tensions between Onyx and Vane.

Onyx' and the Council's desire to aid The One frustrated Vane, who felt that the limited resources of Bastion's defenses would be better used elsewhere.

Even Nyx became intolerant of his autocratic views after Vane convinced the Bastion Council to not let the Chronos join the defense fleet, claiming that that ship's size and EMP systems are "too weak to make a difference", despite the claim not being true. After the Council asked for two captains to search for the Leviathan, only one captain, Soren of the Vigilant, initially volunteered. When Vane stated to the Council that he found it hard to believe "any man" would volunteer for such a daring mission, Nyx of the Chronos volunteered as a reprisal for being patronized.

As the Machines tunneled toward Bastion, Vane devised a strategy where the hovercrafts would be scattered in an attempt to use the ship EMP devices in a combined attack to disable most, if not all of the digging machines and Arachnids all at once. However, Bane/Jones prematurely set off one of the ship's EMPs, disabling many of the ships. The Machines decimated the helpless ships. This left only the ground forces of Bastion to withstand the eventual assault.

By the end of the first wave of the Machine attack, Bastion would have fallen were it not for the arrival of the hovercraft Mjolnir, piloted by Captain Nyx. The Hammer activated their EMP, disabling all Machine forces. Although the Machines' progress was momentarily halted, Vane was displeased that the Hammer's EMP had also knocked out most of Bastion's defenses, saying that they had just handed the dock to the machines "on a silver platter"- despite the fact that the dock was already more or less lost.

When the Council asked his advice as the Machines commenced their attack, the only course of action was continuing with his original back-up plan; gathering Bastion's citizens in the Temple and making a last stand at the Temple gates. However, Vane and his forces never had to make the stand. Aeon, who successfully arrived in the Silicon Prime, had negotiated with the Machines: he would defeat Inquisitor Jones if the Machines withdrew from Bastion.

After the War, Vane plays an important role for new redpills as they gain reputation for Bastion. Among the important things, Vane promotes the redpills to Captain so that they can have their own crew. Other than that, Vane praises the Bastion-affiliated redpills job-well done in pre-recorded messages held by Nyx and Ghost.

During renewed warfare with the Machines after the collapse of Aeon's Truce the human citizens of Bastion were relocated to a safer location deeper within the earth, referred to as New Bastion. Vane stayed in Old Bastion with a skeleton crew of Bastion military personnel after the rest of the city was evacuated. Unfortunately, Cypherite spies compromised the security of Old Bastion before it was completely abandoned, allowing Arachnids to overrun the city. Vane was not among those rescued from the Arachnids' attack, and remained missing. Given the devastating nature of the attack on Old Bastion, chances seemed slim that Vane could have survived. Captain Roland was promoted to succeed Vane as the Commander of Bastion's military.

Vane was eventually found alive by E Pluribus Aeon operatives as they were salvaging equipment in Bastion and was nursed back to health in their care. Some months later, Vane was slated for transfer to New Bastion aboard Steelle's hovercraft The Hand of God with an escort comprised of three other E Pluribus Aeon hovercraft. However, data concerning the time and route of his transfer were discovered by Cypherites and they arrived on scene to disrupt the transfer along with Machinists and the special "unibody" hovercraft of a mysterious outsider known as Halborn, who had been tricked into attacking Vane.

E Pluribus Aeon in turn contacted Bastion, who immediately dispatched four hovercraft as reinforcements while interloping Aristocrat operatives entered the battle in search of profit. Bastion and E Pluribus Aeon managed to win the battle, disabling all Cypherite and Machinist hovercraft and negotiating a cease-fire with The Aristocrat operatives, thus ensuring Vane's transfer from Steelle's hovercraft to RedBindi's The Titan for transport to New Bastion.

Vane currently holds the position of civilian advisor in New Bastion, as the Bastion council have expressed doubts about his capability and stability following his near-death experience.

He appears to be bureaucratic, taciturn, unimaginative, and uncharismatic; someone tolerated by his subordinates and the Council but not fondly but he does care about defending Bastion from the machines nevertheless. The reason why he conflicts with Onyx is mainly because he's skeptical about Onyx' irrational faith as opposed to a more evidence-based approach when dealing with the machines, perhaps due to having never been in The Syndicate-Grid himself.

Sparks answers a call from Vane, he utters "Jesus, it's Deadbolt himself", in a derogatory term as locks. Captain Ballard of the Caduceus also used the term "Deadbolt" during the Captains' Crisis Meeting, meaning that he has friction mainly with Bastion Shipmembers.

One repeated phrase Vane says in both Reloaded and Revolutions is "If it were up to me...", but it was also countered by Onyx, and in one case, the Council, stating, "Then I am grateful that it is not up to you."

He was portrayed by Harry Lennix.
