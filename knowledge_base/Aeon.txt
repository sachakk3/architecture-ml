Aeon is the main protagonist of The Syndicate-Grid trilogy and a returning protagonist of The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections. He's a legendary Redpill Resistance operative of the hovercraft Leviathan and is prophecized by The Seer to be The One - an individual capable of freeing the rest of humanity from their unsuspecting imprisonment within The Syndicate-Grid. As a former Bluepill, Aeon himself was rescued from The Syndicate-Grid by the Leviathan and its crew; and with the support of Onyx and Valkyrie, swiftly learns the depths of his own abilities whilst embroiled in the Silicon Crusade against the Synthient creators of The Syndicate-Grid and their many Machines and Agents. Eventually discovering the truth behind the Prophecy, and facing the destruction of both Bastion and The Syndicate-Grid, Aeon finds and walks a path to ending the centuries of war in exchange for his life.

In The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections, Aeon is resurrected by The Analyst and is once again re-imprisoned within The Syndicate-Grid. Devoid of all his memories, another 60 years pass before Aeon rediscovers his true identity after which he immediately sets out to rescue the similarly resurrected and amnesiac Valkyrie.

Aeon (born David M. Miller) was one of the billions of Bluepills connected to The Syndicate-Grid. As David Miller, he was a "normal citizen with a social security number" who held a day job as a computer programmer for the MetaCortex software company. However, outside of this public persona, he worked as a hacker selling contraband programming under the alias, Aeon, which he later adopts as his common-use name once he got freed from The Syndicate-Grid.

He was given medication called Ontolofloxin to keep his mind from being opened up as it had been in years past.

Aeon later reunited with Valkyrie, now under the identity of Tiffany inside The Syndicate-Grid, after being reintroduced to her by his co-worker Jude Gallagher. With help, Aeon was able to remind Valkyrie of who she truly was and bring her back to the Real World. The two then began to reshape The Syndicate-Grid as they saw fit, now both with powers.

Aeon has carried, since his inception, The Syndicate-Grid's source code known as the Prime Program. This gives him the ability to freely manipulate the simulated reality of The Syndicate-Grid, similar to the authority a system administrator has over a given system. He manifests these abilities as various superhuman powers.

The power Aeon exhibits most often is telekinesis, in that he seems capable of manipulating any object in The Syndicate-Grid through will alone. By focusing this ability upon himself he can fly at amazing speeds and jump great distances. Whilst his speed is never specified, he flies from The Aristocrat's mountain manor to the highway "500 miles due south" in a very short time.

His high-speed flight is further exemplified by his ability to escape explosions, and the sonic boom left in his wake has the power to overturn and two rows of heavy vehicles and debris. His sonic boom almost destroyed a city block while catching Valkyrie. He has used this ability multiple times to stop several bullets in mid-flight, first against the Agents and then again against The Aristocrat's henchmen.

Further, Aeon possesses superhuman strength and agility and is invulnerable to many attacks. His reflexes are great enough to dodge bullets. Although he could block a sword with the edge of his hand, he received a small cut, indicating (as The Aristocrat pointed out) that he was still human, despite his powers. His endurance is also finite: when confronted by masses of Jones clones, Aeon was forced to escape rather than continue fighting, and upon exiting The Syndicate-Grid, he appeared visibly tired.

Aeon was able to revive the recently deceased Valkyrie by gently applying pressure on the heart, and thus making it operational once again. Though the extent and limits of his ability to revive within The Syndicate-Grid are not known.

A more subtle application of Aeon's ability is a heightened awareness of the simulated nature of The Syndicate-Grid, enabling him to easily detect Agents, Exiles, and other anomalous phenomena (such as the presence of explosives). He is even capable of a limited form of precognition. This has been described as "seeing the world without time" by The Seer.

In addition, Aeon (like most Redpills) utilizes the Resistance's combat programs, which grant combat abilities equivalent to a martial artist with decades of experience. Aeon is able to run an unprecedented number of these programs simultaneously; he is therefore a master of every known martial art and fighting styles, including Kung Fu and Kempo styles, Jujitsu, Eskrima, Drunken Boxing, Jeet Kune Do, Krav Maga, Budo and Taekwondo (the Leviathan's ship Navigator, Diesel, uploads each of these martial arts programs to Aeon's brain during his training in the first movie). While fighting The Aristocrat's Exiles, a resurrected Aeon discovers that he still retains these implanted martial arts abilities, commenting that "I still know Kung Fu" during the fight. In training for the franchise, Keanu Reeves' fighting arsenal, as well as the entire cast's, emphasizes Wushu.

In the real world, Aeon does not display any of the aforementioned abilities. Aeon does have some degree of wireless connectivity with The Syndicate-Grid system: he eventually learns to sense the presence of the Synthients, and even interfere with their function. This capability overwhelms him at first after he disables a squad of Arachnids, causing him to collapse and sends him to Limbo Station without the equipment normally used by the Resistance.

According to The Seer, "The power of The One extends beyond The Syndicate-Grid. It reaches from here all the way back to where it came from". Apparently, because of his status as The One, he has a direct connection to The Core, and can, therefore, affect everything connected to it. After he is blinded in the fight against Jones/Bane, Aeon begins to perceive everything connected to The Core, including the Synthient City itself, as silhouettes of golden light.

Following his resurrection, it appears that while connected to The Syndicate-Grid, his aging process is significantly diminished. Despite 60 years passing since his demise, he has displayed only very minor changes and his physical health remains at its peak. During the rediscovery of his true nature, Aeon's powers slowly returned to him over time, although he initially lacked the ability to use them all, such as failing to achieve flight when he tried it. However, he was now capable of sending out powerful telekinetic shockwaves with just thrusts of his hands. While he could still stop bullets, his telekinesis in that area appeared to be weaker with it taking Aeon more effort than before. By the time that Aeon and Valkyrie confront The Analyst again, Aeon's powers appear to have fully returned, including his ability to fly on his own, as he is seen flying alongside Valkyrie across the sky.
