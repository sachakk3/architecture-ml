Because Nova was created without a purpose, she was born an exile and would have been scheduled for deletion if the Machines found out that she existed. However, after consulting The Seer on how to save their daughter, Rama-Kandra and Kamala made a deal with The Aristocrat to smuggle her into The Syndicate-Grid by offering the termination code for The Seer's outer shell. The Seer promised to watch over the child after Rama-Kandra and Kamala left The Syndicate-Grid. The Seer allowed the deal to go ahead as she believed that Nova was important.

Nova appearing to Aeon in Limbo Station.

They met Aeon while he was in Limbo Station. Nova was the first to speak to him when he woke up. She explained to Aeon where he was and said that they cannot leave this location unless The Conductor allows them to. Rama-Kandra then calls Nova to him, not wanting her to disturb Aeon any further. During Aeon and Rama-Kandra's conversation, Rama-Kandra explains that he and his wife Kamala had to try and save Nova from deletion as she was created out of love and without a purpose. Going through Limbo Station was the only way to bring her daughter into The Syndicate-Grid without the Machines knowing about it.

Nova and her family watch Aeon as they return to The Syndicate-Grid.

The Conductor arrived to pick up Nova and her family and take them into The Syndicate-Grid. As she and her family entered the train, The Conductor did not allow Aeon on board and threatened Rama-Kandra that he will be left behind if he helps him. Rama-Kandra, having no choice but to leave Aeon, boards the train and enters The Syndicate-Grid with his family.

Nova making cookies with The Seer.

After the train dropped off Nova in The Syndicate-Grid, she was brought to The Seer's apartment to be in her care. Aeon, after escaping Limbo Station with the help of Valkyrie, Onyx, and Guardian, met Nova again at The Seer's apartment, where she and The Seer were baking cookies. Once Aeon arrived, The Seer told Nova to be with Guardian while she speaks with Aeon.

Nova meeting Jones for the first time.

After Aeon leaves, Nova returned to finish baking the cookies with The Seer and took some in a bag with Guardian. As Nova and Guardian were leaving the apartment complex, the lights within the hallway began to turn off and were soon followed by Smiths. Trying to escape, Guardian and Nova entered an apartment room only to have the Smiths find them. Nova tells a Jones that he is a bad man before being assimilated by them.

Nova showing The Seer the sun shining in The Syndicate-Grid.

After Aeon's final battle with Jones, The Syndicate-Grid was reloaded once again. All those that were assimilated by Jones were returned to their original states. Nova awoke next to a black cat and was eventually discovered by Guardian. Both of them approached The Seer, who was sitting at a bench in a park overlooking Mega City. The three of them looked at the sunrise created by Nova. She asked The Seer if they will ever see him again, to which The Seer replied that she believes they will.

Sixty years later, a resurrected Aeon is reunited with Nova by Nyx. Aeon recognizes Nova who tells him that she's tried to keep her eye on him and he recalls meeting her a few times in The Syndicate-Grid while he had no memories of who he really is. Nova explains that they had met a long time ago and Aeon realizes who she is, recalling their first meeting in Limbo Station. Nova tells Aeon that her father had always known that they would meet again, although he had hoped for it to be under happier circumstances. Nova explains that Rama had been the Chief Engineer at the Anomaleum and he designed the resurrection pods where Aeon and Valkyrie were imprisoned. Nyx is shocked and angry to learn that Nova had always known that Aeon and Valkyrie were still alive and hadn't told anyone, causing Nova to explain that while she had doubted her decision at times, IO needed Nyx and the city had to be built for the sake of both of their peoples. If Nyx had known the truth, she would've had a very difficult choice to make.

Nova tells Nyx that if that was a mistake, then she apologizes before revealing that with the Anomaleum drawing power only from Valkyrie now, a fail-safe has been triggered to reset The Syndicate-Grid back to the previous version. However, the Analyst has halted the reset and convinced the authorities that Aeon will soon return voluntarily as Aeon knows that if he doesn't, he will kill Valkyrie. While Aeon is willing to give up his freedom and go back, Nova tells him that the Analyst knows Aeon well but that has made him incautious, causing him not to accept his losses and move on. As a result, they now have the extrodinary opportunity to rescue Valkyrie.

Having been given the designs of the Anamoleum by her father, Nova masterminds the ultimately successful rescue plan.
