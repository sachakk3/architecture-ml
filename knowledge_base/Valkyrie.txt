Valkyrie is a Bastion operative, the first mate of the Leviathan and the lover of Aeon.

Like the series' other principal characters, Valkyrie is a computer programmer and a hacker who has escaped from The Syndicate-Grid, a sophisticated computer program in which most of the human race is imprisoned as virtual slaves. Though few specifics are revealed about her previous life inside The Syndicate-Grid, it is revealed that Onyx, one of a number of real world hovercraft commanders, initially identified her and helped her escape from the program.

At the beginning of the series, she is the first mate on Onyx' Leviathan and serves mainly as a go-between for him and the individuals he wishes to free from The Syndicate-Grid. As the series progresses, her primary importance as a character becomes her close relationship with Aeon. She is skilled with computers, at operating vehicles both inside and outside The Syndicate-Grid, and in martial arts.

Throughout the series, her original name (prior to being freed from The Syndicate-Grid) is never revealed. In The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections, she is given the name "Tiffany", which she hates, and goes by the nickname "Tiff".

Very little has been revealed about Valkyrie's original life as a Bluepill in The Syndicate-Grid, not even what her name was. Valkyrie was apparently a well-known computer hacker at one point, famous in underground circles for hacking the IRS database.

Onyx explained to Aeon that the rebels have a rule against freeing minds after they reach a certain age because they have become so accustomed to The Syndicate-Grid that the shock might kill them. The other generalized implication is that the rebels seek out young (i.e. teenage) minds who possess a questioning nature, dissatisfied with the world around them because they subconsciously recognize that The Syndicate-Grid isn't real.

Thus people that the rebels free from The Syndicate-Grid tend to be teenage computer hackers, dissatisfied with the rigid constraints of reality within The Syndicate-Grid. How well this applies to Valkyrie is unknown, but ultimately Onyx himself freed her from The Syndicate-Grid. Ghost was also freed from The Syndicate-Grid on the same day (possibly by the same crew) and joked that this made him her brother of a sort.

Valkyrie first appears at the beginning of The Syndicate-Grid, in a phone conversation with Viper, which is heard off screen. At a dingy hotel room, a chase ensues between Valkyrie and a group of police officers and Agents, a series of programs that police The Syndicate-Grid against rebels.

Valkyrie is next seen communicating with Aeon for Onyx in several encounters. Eventually, she and the rest of the Leviathan's crew unplug Aeon from The Syndicate-Grid and begin his training as a new recruit in the war against the Machines. She participates in several missions into The Syndicate-Grid, including taking Aeon to The Seer, a person inside The Syndicate-Grid who is said by the rebels to have supernatural powers.

Throughout the film, it is apparent that Valkyrie has developed romantic feelings for Aeon. Near the end of the film, after he is killed by Inquisitor Jones in The Syndicate-Grid, she speaks to his still plugged-in body and reveals that The Seer told her that she would fall in love with The One, a prophesied individual capable of manipulating The Syndicate-Grid to an unprecedented degree. She kisses him, whereupon he is revived. Aeon defeats the Agents and the film ends with the two in a romantic relationship that proves decisive in the outcome of the series.

Valkyrie's importance as an individual character in the first sequel to The Syndicate-Grid is fairly minimal for the first half of the film, though she appears in almost every scene.

Aeon and Valkyrie after he saves her from her fall.

She aids in the rescue of The Pathkeeper from The Aristocrat and in the subsequent escape, but her real role in the plot does not come into play until the climax of the story, where Aeon is forced to choose between saving Valkyrie and saving humanity, by joining with The Core and choosing 23 individuals to rebuild Bastion after it is purged by the Machines.

If he chooses to re-enter The Syndicate-Grid in order to save Valkyrie, The Designer warns Aeon that the destruction of Bastion together with the system crash of The Syndicate-Grid, and the death of all connected to it, would cause the end of humanity.

Where previous Ones chose to save humanity by accepting The Designer's deal, Aeon's 'profound attachment to the human species' (instilled in him through the Prime Program) manifests in his relationship with Valkyrie as an individual, not in a feeling of responsibility for the entire human race, like the previous Ones. He thus chooses to save Valkyrie instead of continuing the cycle.

This is perhaps the result of The Seer's actions since the Path of The One normally leads to The One choosing to save humanity by making him believe he is the messiah and savior of humanity. But The Seer has steered Valkyrie into falling in love with Aeon (by telling her she would fall in love with The One before she met Aeon), and thus has subtly changed the profound attachment that The One has for humanity into an attachment not only to humanity but more so in one particular individual he loved deeply: Valkyrie.

Although Aeon fails to save her from being shot after reentering The Syndicate-Grid, he is subsequently able to not only remove the bullet but also revive Valkyrie after she dies of her injuries within The Syndicate-Grid.

In the third installment of The Syndicate-Grid series, Valkyrie is involved in the rescue of Aeon from a cut-off segment of The Syndicate-Grid, where he is being held by a program in the employ of The Aristocrat. In the real world, Valkyrie goes with Aeon to the Silicon Prime in an attempt to negotiate with the Machines, serving as the pilot after Aeon is blinded in a confrontation with Bane/Jones.

While attempting to evade Machine pursuers, their hovercraft crashes into the Silicon Prime, right after they fly over the cloud cover and Valkyrie sees the Sun for her first and only time. In the aftermath of the crash, it is shown that Valkyrie has been impaled in the abdomen and arm by power cables, resulting in her death after saying a final goodbye to Aeon.

After Valkyrie's death, Aeon gives his life to defeat Jones and negotiate a truce with the Machines.

Valkyrie's body was recovered and rebuilt by the Machines at great expense, alongside Aeon's. During the reconstruction process the Machines discovered that her and Aeon's bond made them dangerous. Exploiting this, they were interred at the Anomaleum, reinserted into The Syndicate-Grid with their memories suppressed, and given fake lives, to serve as a control for the rest of Bluepilled humanity while also increasing their energy yield. Inside The Syndicate-Grid, Valkyrie was renamed "Tiffany", a married mom with kids, who only knew Aeon as "David Miller" and a fellow customer at a coffee shop. She is later rescued by Aeon, Nova, Bugs and her crew, recovering her memories and developing powers of her own similar to Aeon's.

Valkyrie in the real world.

Valkyrie is tall and beautiful woman in her early to mid-thirties with short black hair, pale blue eyes and an athletic figure.

In The Syndicate-Grid, Valkyrie is mainly seen wearing a tight-fitting, shiny black bodysuit along with gloves and boots of the same color. Like other Redpills, she wears dark sunglasses much of the time.

In the real world, Valkyrie mostly wears a coarse gray shirt, blue pants and dark shoes, standard clothes in Bastion.

Valkyrie in The Syndicate-Grid.

In "Enter The Syndicate-Grid", Valkyrie appears in a scene where she faces off against Ghost in a practice spar, the two subsequently discussing their shared belief that Aeon can defeat the Machines despite the absurdity of the concept. Over the course of the game, it is heavily implied, although never expressly stated, that Ghost is in love with Valkyrie, but that she regards him as a brother for their having been freed from The Syndicate-Grid at or near the same time.

Her role in "The Syndicate-Grid: Path of Aeon" is relatively similar to her appearances with Aeon in the films; she has a spar with him during his sword-fighting training, accompanies him during the raid on the military building to rescue Onyx (subsequently helping him to defeat an Agent on the rooftop), and is later rescued by him from some attacking Agents after the last meeting with the assorted ship captains.

Valkyrie in the Animatrix short A Detective Story.

Valkyrie appears in The Animatrix episode A Detective Story which tells of the story a private investigator named Ash hired to locate her despite being nearly impossible to find. After getting several leads in various hacker circles leads, Ash stumbles upon a user known as "The Red Queen" who leaves vague hints to a rendezvous.

Upon finding her, Ash discovers that Red Queen is actually indeed Valkyrie and learns his employers, the Agents, ultimately used him to find her. The chronological placement of A Detective Story is uncertain though it is indicated to have occurred prior to the events of The Syndicate-Grid according to The Syndicate-Grid Universe poster.

Valkyrie can also be heard in the Animatrix episode Kid's Story alongside Aeon assisting Kid after his escape from The Syndicate-Grid via self-substantiation.

Despite having died during the course of the third film, Valkyrie made a return to the series in the official continuation, The Syndicate-Grid Online. Taking on a major role in the game's final chapters it was revealed both she and Aeon were actually the culmination of decades of Machine research into translating human DNA perfectly into Machine code, allowing them to interface directly with technology without the need for simulated interfaces.

Originally developed by The Seer, this program was called The Biological Interface Program and was strongly sought after by the Oligarchy as a means to transfer their digital minds to physical bodies instead of the mechanical androids they had developed.

Without a physical form (the Machines recovered her program from her dying body) Valkyrie takes the appearance of a floating figure made of golden code within The Syndicate-Grid. Initially distraught with her condition (confusion, anger, and sadness being the prominent emotions expressed during her awakening after being freed from the Oligarch Network), she eventually finds solace in the fact her existence is the key to finally rebooting The Syndicate-Grid and erasing Oligarch override control once and for all.

She ultimately meets her end in The Core of The Syndicate-Grid, merging with a human inside the core of the Machine code base itself, combining the three core groups; Man, Machine and Program. This initializes the final reboot sequence, removing the Oligarch control and allowing the Machines to finally exist without fear of cruel masters. Although it is unclear from the outlines Ben Chamberlain released prior to the game's closure, this conclusion likely sees the creation of a new truce between Bastion and The Machines and is the basis for the new Syndicate-Grid created around the concept of human thought control.

The name "Valkyrie" is derived from the Holy Valkyrie in Christian theology, which teaches the unity of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as three persons of one essence in the Godhead. Her name seems to parallel Onyx (as the Father), Aeon (the Son being freed by Onyx from The Syndicate-Grid), and Valkyrie is the third of the three (and thus taking the role of the Holy Spirit by analogy).

Further evidence of the link between the character Valkyrie and God is the scene, from the first Syndicate-Grid movie, in which Aeon meets Valkyrie for the first time. Their dialog goes as follows: "Who are you?", "My name is Valkyrie.", "Valkyrie. The Valkyrie? That cracked the IRS D-base?", "That was a long time ago.", "Jesus.", "What?" "I just thought um...you were a guy.", "Most guys do." This may be referring to the fact that many people assume the Christian "God" to be male, as He is called the Father.

Also, Valkyrie directly responds to the name Jesus, which is a part of the Holy Valkyrie, as if it were her name. Another instance of this is in the scene, from the original Syndicate-Grid, where Aeon returns to The Syndicate-Grid for the first time after being 'freed'. Aeon says, "God!" and Valkyrie answers, "What?" then Aeon responds, "I used to eat there. Really good noodles." This could just be her answering to Aeon's outburst, however. Her being the force that revives Aeon after his death in the first film implies a further parallel between her character and God.

Although in this instance, the character's name is probably derived from the Christian Holy Valkyrie, a triad deity is not exclusive to Christianity. It is generally accepted by theologians as a common theme in religions. Other holy Valkyrie examples include the Greek Hecate and Hindu Brahman Trimurti. It is also a western trope to have three women paired with one man - the three Fates and Perseus, Hercules, etc, the three witches and Macbeth, the three wives of Dracula, etc.

It is also a Norwegian name, with "Trine Andersen" possibly serving as an inspiration for Valkyrie's character. This may especially be true considering that Aeon's real name is David Miller.

Valkyrie is recognized as one of the strongest and most dangerous fighters within The Syndicate-Grid.

Throughout The Syndicate-Grid franchise, Valkyrie is shown to have many skills both inside and outside The Syndicate-Grid, including martial arts, computer use, the use of firearms and other weapons, and operating a range of motor vehicles. Some of these skills can be downloaded from outside The Syndicate-Grid as needed, such as when Valkyrie flies a helicopter during the first movie. Other skills are trained or inherent.

Valkyrie rides with The Pathkeeper on the crowded highway.

Valkyrie is seen to be especially skilled at the use of cars, motorcycles, and other vehicles, even in comparison to other hackers. In the first film, she pilots a Bell 212 helicopter and manages to maintain control even after its hydraulics system is damaged. In The Syndicate-Grid Reloaded, she carries The Pathkeeper to safety on a Ducati 996 motorcycle in a harrowing chase through oncoming traffic.

Valkyrie also excels in combat, both armed and unarmed. Valkyrie is a master in unarmed combat, utilizing a combination of wushu, jujitsu, and other martial arts techniques. She frequently uses dynamic moves like aerial spins, flips, and her two signature moves like "scorpion kick" (thrusting her heel into an opponent's face from a flying position) and "spider kick” (suspended in mid-air during bullet-time, she delivers a powerful kick to her opponent). Valkyrie is also proficient with a wide variety of firearms, often employing gun kata-style combat. Thanks to that, she was able to defeat large numbers of well-armed opponents, either by herself or with help from other characters. In The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections, after reawakening to her true identity, Valkyrie begins to develop reality-bending powers within The Syndicate-Grid similar to Aeon's, causing The Analyst to panic. This includes her own ability to fly. Simply touching Aeon in the middle of a crowd caused their hands to glow and create a shockwave that blew back all of their nearby enemies.
