Bastion is the last, only known human city on the planet Earth. The city is the launching point for The Resistance against the Machines that threaten their freedom.

Bastion, a city of over 250,000 humans, is actually a massive series of caverns deep under the ruined planet's surface, close to the planet's core, for warmth, power, and protection. Based on the understanding of the human government living there, the location of Bastion is unknown to the Machines, until the Machines found a way into it through the Dock.

The people of Bastion believe that they had fought the Machines for nearly 100 years, as told by Onyx in a speech made prior to the Machines' final assault on the city.

The humans of Bastion believe that their city was built shortly after the destruction of the above-ground cities of man and the creation of The Syndicate-Grid. Their history tells of The One, who freed the first humans from the hold of The Syndicate-Grid. The current year is unknown but Onyx states it to be closer to 2199, as opposed to Aeon's 1999.

The Designer tells the story differently. According to The Designer, Bastion is in fact a place allowed to exist by the Machines as a form of control. Although 1% of the population subconsciously reject The Syndicate-Grid, only a small fraction of that 1% are actually freed from The Syndicate-Grid to become a citizen of Bastion.

This is based on the population of Bastion being around 250,000, while the population of The Syndicate-Grid is in the billions (per Inquisitor Jones's quote about billions of people living out their lives in The Syndicate-Grid). The vast number of people still hardwired to The Syndicate-Grid who have subconsciously rejected The Syndicate-Grid, but have not been presented with a redpill live out their lives with a splinter in the mind--always feeling like they are living in a dream.

Bastion is a control measure designed specifically as a society expecting a messiah figure. This expectation, which is created by The Seer's prophecy, helps to ensure that when the next One arrives on the scene that they will treat him as a messiah and cause him to eventually believe that he is The One.

This is because of The Designer's need for The One to take the code he carries (consisting of the rejections of 1% of the population) to The Core to be disseminated. This temporarily brings The Syndicate-Grid to 100% acceptance, which will allow The Designer to perform a reload of the system.

The real threat to The Syndicate-Grid is not Bastion, but the systemic anomaly created by 1% of the population subconsciously rejecting The Syndicate-Grid. Bastion's location is always known by The Designer, as it is rebuilt in the exact same location as the previous Bastion. With each iteration, the Machines get better and better at destroying it. It is allowed to exist only as long as it is needed. Once The One is found, trained and sent on the Path of The One to The Designer's chamber, Bastion is scheduled for destruction. Most of the Agents of the system are not privy to this information and act out accordingly.

When The One reaches The Core to disseminate the integral anomaly, he reinserts the Prime Program, which is The Designer's way of referring to the Path of The One. The Path of The One is a subprogram within the larger programming of The Syndicate-Grid that ends with The One's arrival at The Core. It is a control measure to ensure that each cycle begins and ends with The One.

After disseminating the integral anomaly at The Core, The One reinserts the Path of The One into the programming of The Syndicate-Grid by choosing 23 individuals to rebuild Bastion. Because of Aeon's actions at the end of the The Syndicate-Grid Revolutions, the Prime Program was not reinserted at the time that The Syndicate-Grid was reloaded.

When The One arrives at The Designer's chamber on his search for The Core, The Designer tells The One the history of The Syndicate-Grid. By this time The One will have come to believe that he is the savior of humankind and will do what he needs to in order to save it.

He is told that he can either allow a fraction of 1% of the population to die while the rest continue to live in The Syndicate-Grid, or he can allow all of humankind to die. Inevitably The One will choose to save humanity and will follow the Path of The One to The Core. Aeon is unique in that he loves Valkyrie more than he loves humanity, and is willing to sacrifice humanity in order to save her.

Bastion, according to The Designer, has been completely destroyed five times by the time of the sixth One, Aeon, meets The Designer. As a result, the actual year on Earth is estimated to be closer to 2699, not 2199. With the events of The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections taking place sixty years later, it would be somewhere around 2758 in the real world.

Unlike his predecessors, Aeon defies The Designer's ultimatum and chooses to save Valkyrie from certain death. In doing so, he reveals a new truth about the Prophecy: it is, in fact, real. The Seer gambles on Aeon and Valkyrie's love to break the cycle of destruction for Bastion by aiding Aeon to realize that his mission was not to meet with The Designer, but to travel to the Silicon Prime itself and confront The Core.

As Aeon heads for the city, Bastion is besieged by hundreds of thousands of Arachnids, tearing through Bastion's defenses and readying to eradicate the populace and Bastion's existence yet again, for a sixth time. The Arachnids are instructed to stop their attack moments before they would break through to the living area of the city: Aeon is in negotiation with the Deus Ex Machina at the time to save the city, and brokers a peace on the condition that Aeon removes Jones from The Syndicate-Grid.

After Aeon successfully destroys Jones and his clones within The Syndicate-Grid, the Arachnids retreat, The Syndicate-Grid is reloaded, Bastion is saved, and the cycle is broken.

During the Machine Civil War Bastion was attacked and destroyed by a faction of Machines. Human survivors alongside Machine allies built a new city called IO.
