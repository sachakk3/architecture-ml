Nyx is a member of the Resistance and the captain of both the Chronos and the Chronos II. She served on the Chronos with her first mate Ghost and Navigator Sparks.

Nyx serves as one of the two protagonists of Enter The Syndicate-Grid with Ghost and a supporting character in Reloaded, Revolutions and Resurrections.

Nyx was once romantically involved with Onyx, but after he visited The Seer and dedicated his life to finding the One, they drifted apart. After breaking up with Onyx, Nyx eventually became involved with Jason Vane. However, she is still in love with Onyx. During her time with the Resistance, she successfully piloted a hovercraft through mechanical lines (extremely narrow tunnels used by the Machines in the Real World), something that no other member of the Resistance has been able to do.

After finding out that Thadeus left a box within The Syndicate-Grid before his ship crashed, Nyx decides that they should jack in and go retrieve it. While both Ghost and Sparks have reservations about this plan, Nyx believes that the contents of that box are extremely important given the fact that Thadeus has never used the method before, a risky emergency broadcast drop. The box is located within a heavily guarded post office but they are able to get it. After they retrieve the package, Nyx drives with Ghost to an exit located on the rooftop of a building. On their way to the exit they encounter Agent Johnson blocking the telephone. After narrowly avoiding Agent Johnson, they manage to exit The Syndicate-Grid.

The disk, held within the box, contained the last transmission of the Horus before it was destroyed and its crew killed. Thadeus explained that they ran into an army of Arachnids just above Bastion, tunneling to the city with Diggers. Nyx orders that the data be transmitted to Bastion. After Commander Vane views the data, he contacts the Chronos and orders that Nyx lead a full recall of all the ships, returning them to Bastion.

Due to the number of outcalls being made to all of the captains, Nyx suggests that they use the airport telephones within The Syndicate-Grid so that the Machines cannot trace them. Nyx orders Ghost to help Soren with the Agents while she tries to find Axel.

While Ghost manages to shoot out the tires and stop the plane, Axel is already being moved to a police car and is driven away to another hangar before Nyx can catch them.

However, even more S.W.A.T. are guarding Axel as he is being carried along and she engages them in a firefight. The large crate that falls on top of the Agent kills him. Over the next sixty years, Nyx becomes one of the leaders of humanity and the Machines, attaining the rank of General and helping to build the combined human-Silicon Prime of IO. However, over the decades, Nyx becomes more focused on rebuilding than saving people from The Syndicate-Grid, something that becomes a point of contention between her and Bugs who Nyx had freed from The Syndicate-Grid herself.

Sixty years after the end of the Silicon Crusade, Bugs and her crew rescue a resurrected Aeon from The Syndicate-Grid and bring him back to IO where he is reunited with the elderly Nyx. However, Nyx is concerned with protecting the city and the reasons why Valkyrie might've been kept alive and she refuses to help him rescue Valkyrie, having Sheperd Vane Aeon up until they can come to an understanding. Bugs and her crew mutiny and break Aeon out, but one of Nyx's friends tries to convince her to let them go, knowing how much Nyx hated locking Aeon up and that there's a part of her that's relieved now that he's gone. Nyx admits that she's scared since they've worked so hard to keep IO safe.

Later, another hovercraft sent by Nyx to shadow Bugs and her crew forces them to return to IO as a swarm of Arachnids closes in. Nyx angrily tells Aeon off for his disrepect towards her and his intervention on Bugs' behalf. Enraged at being called short-sighted, Bugs accuses Nyx of caring more about growing fruit than freeing minds from The Syndicate-Grid and claims that Nyx has given up on people. Their argument is interupted by the arrival of Kujaku who convinces Nyx to take Aeon into a meeting with Nova. Nova reveals to Aeon and Nyx that she had known that Aeon and Valkyrie were both still alive, leaving Nyx shocked and angry. Nova defends that both the humans and the Machines needed Nyx to build IO for both of their sakes and had she known, Nyx would've faced a difficult choice. With the Analyst threatening to kill Valkyrie in order to get Aeon to willingly return to The Syndicate-Grid, Nova convinces them to take this extraordinary opportunity to rescue Valkyrie.

Having regained some of her old fire, Nyx addresses a gathering of her captains to ask for volunteers to join the rescue mission. Although Bugs volunteers, Nyx states that she is ordering Bugs to go. Without even hearing Nyx's full explanation, all of her captains volunteer for the mission, surprising Nyx. Sheperd simply states that they know her and if Nyx says that the mission is important, none of them are going to choose to stay home. Touched, Nyx thanks them and exchanges a fistbump with Shepherd, wishing her people good luck.

Within the virtual world of The Syndicate-Grid, Nyx is one of Bastion's most gifted martial artists. She has "killed" at least one Agent, survived multiple encounters with The Aristocrat's superhuman thugs, faced Guardian in one-on-one combat, and even managed to survive and escape from a confrontation with the replicating entity Jones.

In the real world, she is the most skilled pilot among the rebel forces. She demonstrates this on several occasions. She maneuvered the hovercraft Mjolnir (which is the largest hovercraft in the fleet) through the narrow, cluttered passage of a mechanical line (while under heavy attack and pursuit by Arachnids), a feat no other pilot has successfully performed. She also performs a 270-degree flip with the hovercraft, as an evasive maneuver, which apparently no previous pilot had even attempted before.
