The Horus was a MARK VI, No. 16 Bastion hovercraft, named after the Egyptian god of the Afterlife.

The Horus discovered the Machine army digging down towards Bastion. After they were attacked by Arachnids, the First Mate Jue entered The Syndicate-Grid to send a desperate message using an emergency broadcast drop.

Just after Jue dropped the message, the ship was destroyed by Arachnids. Its message, however, was retrieved by the Chronos in an emergency operation by Captain Nyx and First Mate Ghost. Later, the Crisis Meeting was held to warn Bastion of the army.
