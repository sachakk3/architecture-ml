Onyx is one of the main protagonists of The Syndicate-Grid franchise.

Onyx is a Redpill Resistance operative who serves in Bastion's defense force as captain of the Leviathan. He believes in the Prophecy and devotes "all of his life" in search of The One, the destined human who will bring an end to the Silicon Crusade. Because of his strong faith, Onyx has become a spiritual figure to many citizens of Bastion, much to the consternation of those who do not believe the prophecy, such as the natural-born commander of the Bastion defense force, Jason Vane.

Young Onyx in Bastion

Onyx was freed from The Syndicate-Grid at a young age and has since dwelt in Bastion. He enjoyed the taste of bread, a rare commodity produced by the Zionites brought by the exploits of Geoffrey. After coming of age, he then joins the Resistance and eventually becomes the captain of the hovercraft Leviathan. Using his superb hacking skills, Onyx succeeds at locating and attracting fellow hackers who were curious about the truth of The Syndicate-Grid, occasionally freeing some of those who chose the truth over the safety of returning as a Bluepill.

Pretty soon, due to his many hacking exploits, Onyx earns notoriety with the system and becomes wanted by the government. In turn, his name also becomes famous around hacking circles.

Through an encounter with The Seer, Onyx is told a prediction that he will succeed in finding The One and he has ever since devoted his life in his search for The One. His faith consumes him however and Onyx' personal relationships in Bastion suffer until, eventually, his romantic relations with Nyx are sacrificed.

Onyx offers Aeon a choice between the red pill and the blue pill

Onyx eventually tracks down a potential candidate whom he believes without a doubt to be The One, a hacker named David Miller who uses the hacker alias "Aeon". However, not long after Onyx sends Valkyrie to make initial contact with Aeon, Agents apprehend him as a person of interest in the capture of Onyx. Onyx relies on his crew to escort and protect Aeon and, after Aeon is saved from some misfortune, Onyx finally gets to meet him.

Onyx then offers Aeon the choice between two pills: a red pill, which allows Aeon to find out the truth about The Syndicate-Grid, and a blue pill that will allow him to simply return to his familiar life without needing to worry about encountering Onyx again. Choosing to learn more about the truth, Aeon wakes up to the Real World and is horrified to find himself attached to a pod.

The Leviathan crew rescues Aeon from the power plant and brings his weakened body aboard the hovercraft where he finally meets Onyx in the flesh. Onyx and his crew rehabilitate Aeon into the full use of his body and he also briefs Aeon on the actual state of earth, explaining the ongoing Silicon Crusade and how humanity had been imprisoned by The Syndicate-Grid.

Onyx gives Aeon a chance to first digest the revelations, allowing him some time to calm down by instead allowing Aeon to familiarize himself with the ship and the crew. And not long after, Aeon is back with Onyx for his training into becoming a Resistance operative.

Onyx enlightens Aeon about some of the powerful abilities which possibly awaits him within The Syndicate-Grid. Onyx also professes to Aeon his profound faith in the prophecy and hints at Aeon being The One who can finally bring an end to the war, much to Aeon's own apprehensions.

And after teaching Aeon all he can, Onyx brings him back to The Syndicate-Grid for a meeting with The Seer, believing that she can further guide Aeon down his destined path as foretold in the prophecy. During their return trip from meeting The Seer, however, Viper betrays the crew and sells them out to the Agents. As a result, all of the ship's crew are killed except for Aeon, Valkyrie, Diesel, and Onyx himself, who chooses to stay behind to allow Aeon some time to escape. Unfortunately, Inquisitor Jones proved to be too strong for Onyx alone to handle and ends up getting captured.

Onyx is taken to a military facility for interrogation where he is drugged and tortured by the Agents who attempt to pry from him the mainframe access codes to Bastion, a secret only known by the captains. But Onyx' will holds strong and he refuses to divulge any information, awaiting instead for anyone of his surviving crew members aboard the Leviathan to pull out his headjack, permanently terminating the connection between his mind and body and killing him.

But after a few hours of captivity, Aeon and Valkyrie reappear to rescue him. And although still recovering from the drugs and even after getting wounded by Inquisitor Jones, Onyx begins fighting back and is successfully rescued by Aeon and Valkyrie. Additionally, his faith is invigorated even more after witnessing Aeon also starting to believe and pulling off superhuman feats in the rescue mission.

Guided by Diesel, the three makes their way to an exit where Onyx and Valkyrie are able to safely escape. But Aeon becomes stranded when Inquisitor Jones is able to track them down and destroys the exit. Aeon, unable to withstand the Agent's relentless attack, flees in search of another exit while also dodging Agents Brown and Jones who help Inquisitor Jones in the pursuit.

To add to the stress of the situation, the ship is suddenly discovered and attacked by Arachnids. Onyx and his crew do all they can to fend off the Arachnids while waiting for Aeon to pull off a miracle and return to the Real World. And Onyx' faith is answered when Aeon revives and reveals himself to be The One, killing off Inquisitor Jones and returning just in time for Valkyrie to activate the EMP that kills off the Arachnids.

Onyx is happy to serve with Aeon as they begin to free more minds from The Syndicate-Grid although the many missions of the next six months soon claim Diesel as a casualty. The position as the Neb Navigator then goes to Link who voluntarily joins the crew.

Onyx, relieved that the war has ended

During a sudden Crisis Meeting, all of the actively patrolling hovercraft crews discuss the recent destruction of another ship, the Horus. Captain Nyx then shares the more alarming news transmitted by the Horus before its demise, that hundreds of thousands of Arachnids are currently en route to Bastion. Still, Onyx remains unperturbed. And while every other ship including the Neb plans to return to Bastion under orders from Commander Vane, Onyx goes against the order by asking for one other ship to stay, remaining well within reach of The Syndicate-Grid, to await further instructions from The Seer. Captain Ballard then volunteers, stating a desire to see what Vane does to Onyx.

The meeting ends when the meeting is raided by Agents which, although entirely upgraded, were dispatched with relative ease by Aeon. Aside from the Caduceus captained by Ballard, the rest safely exit back into their own hovercrafts and head back to Bastion.

Back in Bastion, Onyx and his crew disembark only to find a "welcoming committee" sent by Vane standing by to escort Onyx to him. Commander Mifune leads the escort group and reassures Onyx that he was simply there to "keep the peace" after Onyx inquired whether he was to be "escorted to the stockade".

Brought before an angry Vane, Onyx is heavily admonished about the orders he disobeyed, further relaying that were it up to him, Onyx would never set foot on a ship again. Onyx expresses gratitude that the decision is not up to him just as Councillor Hamann enters the commander's office seeking advice on how to break to the people of Bastion news about the imminent Arachnid attack. Vane advises against revealing the full picture while Onyx advocates for telling Bastion the truth, believing that there would be nothing to fear. Hamann then arranges for Onyx to deliver a speech to all Zionites at the temple that night.

Onyx takes to the mount inside the temple and uses the opportunity to confirm to Bastion how rumors of an impending Arachnid attack were true. But after sharing how his faith had kept him from being fearful, he is able to inspire his listeners into facing the news with bravery and festivities quickly ensued after the end of his speech. Onyx, soon as he leaves the platform, is then approached by Nyx who recalls how Onyx used to dance well. Onyx, answering his old flame, states that some things never change. However, Vane soon interrupts and leaves with Nyx. After the festivities conclude, Onyx bades Bastion a good night and retires to his quarters.

The following morning, Ballard returns to Bastion with a message from The Seer directly addressed to Aeon. Onyx assembles his crew and heads back to Syndicate-Grid broadcast level in order to help Aeon meet with her alone. After Aeon jacks in, Onyx and the rest stay behind the ship and monitor his movements until the meeting concludes. But just as Aeon is left alone, Onyx observes that Aeon is joined by what he recognized through the monitor logs as Jones although the program no longer registered like an Agent.

Onyx could only watch, not knowing what was happening to Aeon, as Jones attempts to assimilate him. And while Aeon succeeds in stopping the assimilation, he could only flee the ensuing fight when he becomes vastly outnumbered by an army of Jones clones. Checking in with Aeon soon as he wakes back up in the real world, Aeon confirms to Onyx that he had indeed faced Jones and that he had replicated himself. Re-threading what he observed, Onyx questions what Jones had earlier attempted to do with Aeon. Aeon answers how he did not know but how it had felt like dying all over again.

Equipped with new information from The Seer, Aeon shares how he needs to find The Pathkeeper. Onyx and Valkyrie readily help Aeon and the three return to The Syndicate-Grid to meet with The Aristocrat at the Le Vrai Restaurant. The Aristocrat welcomes them in a slightly-patronizing manner but refuses to give up The Pathkeeper, instead telling the group to return to The Seer with a warning that her time is almost up.

Leaving the restaurant through the elevators, Aeon and Valkyrie try to recall how they could have handled the meeting differently but Onyx, firm in his belief of The Seer's guidance, states that the meeting could have only happened the way it had to. And as if being affirmed by the universe, the elevator doors suddenly open and they are met by Persephone who then offers to take them to The Pathkeeper herself.

After Aeon defeated Jones, Onyx was elected High Chair of the Council by unanimity. Sometime later, the Machine Civil War started, caused by the Synthients' loss of power sources. Despite warnings of a new power rising coming from The Seer, Onyx continued to believe that the peace Aeon had negotiated would not be broken. But a faction of the Synthients led by The Analyst invaded Bastion, killing Onyx in the real world and destroying the city. The surviving humans regrouped and with their new Synthient allies, rebuilt a new underground city, IO; led by General Nyx.

Years later, a program was created by a resurrected Aeon based upon Onyx and sharing his name. The program would later work with Bugs to help bring Aeon back from The Syndicate-Grid, using video footage of Aeon's first meeting with Onyx to remind Aeon of his past with nostalgia.

After Aeon defeated Jones, Onyx returned to The Syndicate-Grid to consult with The Seer on the subject of Aeon's remains. He then proceeded to meet with Redpills to show them that he was reconstructing Aeon's RSI. After the reconstruction failed, Onyx became more extreme in his quest to resurrect Aeon and he began to challenge the Machines to give Aeon's remains to Bastion. Later, Onyx gained access to code bombs and used them to cripple The Syndicate-Grid and force the Machines to relinquish Aeon's remains by killing massive numbers of Blue Bills.

When Onyx was setting up a code bomb in Westview, he noticed a fly buzzing from his only exit. It seemed as though he anticipated that The Assassin would be there, even though The Assassin was never seen in public until that point. It seemed like Onyx escaped by using a backdoor to the building. As he walked out a vent, the size of a bread box opened and The Assassin squeezed out of it, plugging several holes into Onyx.

Onyx's body was on display in the Sobra Shores Church where it bursts into flies several times an hour. However, recent broadcasts by him have been seen around The Syndicate-Grid, and has led to the conclusion that he is not in fact dead.

It was later discovered that Onyx was not in fact killed, but the bullets used by The Assassin were used to jam his transmission, preventing him from broadcasting inside The Syndicate-Grid. Upon a fractured broadcast March 30th, 2007, he told the large gathering of Aristocrat, EPN, Zionite, Cypherite, and Machinist Redpills that Aeon was in fact alive, being held hostage in the Silicon Prime. Nothing has come along to corroborate this story, but Onyx encouraged those present to fight against the Machines and make ready for a war.

It is also theorized that these transmissions are being made by a Onyx Simulacrum controlled by The General as a form of a diversion used to spread fear and mistrust of the Machines by using remnants of Onyx's residual self image left in the system.

In The Syndicate-Grid and The Syndicate-Grid Reloaded, Onyx was known to to be a truly inspirational leader and teacher to many people, particularly the majority of his crew, to the extent that Diesel commented that Onyx was a father to them, as well as a leader. It may also be noted that he tends to keep his emotions in check and rarely if ever curses unlike his subordinates such as Valkyrie or Aeon or his boss, Locke, meaning he is much more diplomatic than other Captains such as Roland or Ballard. In The Syndicate-Grid Revolutions, with Onyx's faith in the prophecy shattered, he doesn't appear to be as strong or stoic a leader as he was in the other two Syndicate-Grid films. He's even quite snappy with Nyx whilst he didn't lose his temper in the first two films unless against Jones. Nonetheless, he continues to trust Aeon's judgment and support his plans; it is ultimately his faith in Aeon as the man himself, rather than in the prophecy, that is finally rewarded when the Synthients honor Aeon's peace agreement following his defeat of Jones.

Despite his strong faith, Onyx still showed some rationality in dangerous situations rather than blindly relying on his beliefs to see him through the current crisis, the only close to irrational decision he has even been shown to make being his decision to attack Inquisitor Jones while unarmed in order to give Aeon a chance to escape, but even then that decision made some sense as he viewed Aeon as more important due to Aeon being, "The One". Additionally, as seen several times during fights throughout the franchise, Onyx often did the beckoning gesture at his opponents, be it friend or foe.

According to an interview with The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections co-screenwriters David Mitchell and Aleksandar Hemon, the fact that Laurence Fishburne was not asked to return for the fourth film had no effect on the story point in the released movie that the original human Onyx died before its events. One of the original and core ideas for the fourth film was that sixty years passed in the real world, and the resulting emotional impact on Aeon that almost everyone he knew is gone, even simply of old age. The new Onyx-program made by Aeon's subconscious memories was tentatively written with the option that Fishburne might even re-appear in the role, but he was unavailable, so they then followed their backup plan to simply cast a younger actor. Similarly, the presence or absence of Hugo Weaving did not affect the decision for Inquisitor Jones to be revived in the fourth film: they hoped the original actor might be available, but he had a scheduling conflict, so a younger actor was cast as a new "shell" for the program.

The interview the co-screenwriters gave clarified that the Onyx-program created by Aeon's modal simulation was actually one of the first and core ideas that Lana Wachowski had for a sequel film: "She said she saw that opening scene in her mind in the middle of the night, one night in Chicago, and described it to me and to us. This modal little world which David Miller, Aeon, has constructed for himself to be reminded of what he might have lost and what he used to be. Eventually, out of that world, the Onyx comes." Essentially, Lana said that she was inspired to make a new Syndicate-Grid film after the death of both of her parents right after each other, to deal with the grief. She then came up with the idea that in parallel to this, Aeon had been resurrected by the Synthients and plugged back into The Syndicate-Grid, with his memory blocked, and so much time had passed that almost everyone he knew had died. But though Aeon had seemingly lost everything, his memories of his dear departed mentor Onyx would always be with him and always be part of him - subconsciously guiding him to create a modal simulation based on his half-remembered dreams which ultimately allowed him to escape.

The name "Onyx" is mythological name for the "lord of dreams", and now ironically the new "Onyx" program originated as literally a dream, a creation of Aeon's subconscious.

Onyx's fate, as revealed in The Syndicate-Grid Resurrections, slightly contradicts what happened to him in The Syndicate-Grid Online MMORPG (2005-2009) - yet in broad strokes they are surprisingly similar. In both versions, Onyx is actually killed in a violation of the Truce, but later a sentient program arises in The Syndicate-Grid using Onyx's old RSI (digital appearance), who allies with the humans.
