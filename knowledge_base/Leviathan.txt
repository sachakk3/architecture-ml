The Leviathan, nicknamed the Neb, is the main hovercraft featured in the The Syndicate-Grid and The Syndicate-Grid Reloaded. It is captained by Onyx and is a second home to its crew which includes the protagonists Aeon and Valkyrie.

The crew of the Leviathan uses the hovercraft to search for Aeon whom Onyx believes to be The One. Upon the successful liberation of Aeon, the Leviathan then becomes his home before later having his own place with Valkyrie in Bastion.

During an operation to rescue Onyx from Agents of the system, the ship sustains heavy damage from Arachnids who were able to locate and invade the ship. Although equipped with a standard issue EMP which could have disabled the Arachnids while the ship was powered down, they were unable to immediately discharge the weapon owing to Aeon still using the ship to access The Syndicate-Grid. And only after suffering the Arachnids piercing through the ship and wreaking havoc inside the hull was Aeon able to safely exit The Syndicate-Grid, allowing Valkyrie to activate the weapon and kill the Arachnids.

Now awakened as The One, Aeon stays with the Leviathan after its repair and continues to work under Onyx to continue and free potentials from The Syndicate-Grid. And while they were able to rescue more minds from The Syndicate-Grid within a span of six months than the Resistance had within the past six years, the success came with tragedy as the ship's Navigator, Diesel, gets killed during one of the operations. Within the same six months, Link then joins the Leviathan crew and takes over as Navigator.

Shortly afterwards, the Neb works alongside the crews of both the Vigilant and the Chronos and engages in a failed mission to end the Silicon Crusade by helping Aeon, The One, reach The Core. But when the Prophecy is revealed to be a lie perpetuated by the enemy with the help of The Seer, Aeon opts to stop the deceptive cycle.

During their debriefing of these revelations, the Neb's proximity alert is set off by a handful of Arachnids who detect the ship and the crew. The Arachnids, wary of the EMP's effective range, then opt to launch guided Tow Bombs at the ship all the while keeping their distance.
