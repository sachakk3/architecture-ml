The Seer is a program with such insight into human psychology that she has clairvoyant abilities. She was initially created to interpret aspects of the human psyche and was responsible for the key principles behind the 7th "current" version of The Syndicate-Grid. Having served her purpose, she went into Exile and aided The Resistance in the Silicon Crusade; she ultimately is responsible for ending the war through her manipulation of key members within The Resistance, including Aeon.

In the theatrical releases, she is portrayed by actress Gloria Foster in the first and second films, and then Mary Alice in the third film and in the video game adaptations Enter The Syndicate-Grid and The Path of Aeon.

The Seer baking cookies.

The Seer appears as an African American woman in her late 60s. She frequently enjoys making cookies and likes the smell they make while they are cooking; she also has a penchant for candy. The Seer aids the human resistance in freeing humanity from the oppression of the Machines. According to Onyx, The Seer has been in the service of The Resistance "since the beginning."

The Seer gives predictions and insight to Bastion operatives who choose to hear her. The One prediction she relays to The Resistance of Bastion is that of the Prophecy, where the war will end when The One returns to end the hold of The Syndicate-Grid.

For Onyx, The Seer tells him that he would find The One, a human/program hybrid with incredible abilities within The Syndicate-Grid who could manipulate and at times rewrite the code and who'd ultimately end the Silicon Crusade.

The Seer's powers of precognition appear amazingly accurate, even knowing that Aeon was about to knock over a vase in her kitchen. She refers to a Latin saying on her wall "know thyself" - a popular tenet of Greek life. She leaves Aeon in doubt as to his true path, noting that he has the "gift", but appears to be waiting for something: "Your next life, maybe." This presumably meant the moment where Aeon dies and comes back to life at the end of The Syndicate-Grid, aware of his powers as The One. She also predicts a time when Aeon will have to choose between his life and that of his leader, Onyx. One of them will be saved, but the other will die.

The Seer's prediction leads Aeon to rescue Onyx, but lose his own life at the hands of Inquisitor Jones in the process. But Valkyrie intervenes. She confesses to Aeon's lifeless body in the real world that The Seer had said she would fall in love with The One, which meant that Aeon could not actually be dead. With a kiss from Valkyrie, Aeon's consciousness rejects the idea that he has actually died, and he re-awakens within The Syndicate-Grid, enlightened, and empowered as The One.

The Seer offering Aeon a cookie after telling him about his "choice".

In a later meeting, Aeon can now see that The Seer is not a human at all, but yet another program within The Syndicate-Grid, perhaps yet another form of control over humanity. But The Seer warns Aeon to make up his own mind as to whether she was a friend or enemy. She directs Aeon to find the entryway to The Core by finding The Pathkeeper, a program that can open hidden and locked backdoors within The Syndicate-Grid. Unfortunately, The Pathkeeper is being held by what The Seer describes as "a very dangerous program, one of the oldest of us, he is called The Aristocrat."

While Aeon is successful in reaching The Core, he meets with The Designer, who tells him that The Seer, a sentient program designed to understand human psychology, is The Syndicate-Grid's "mother", for lack of a better term. Aeon also learns that the Prophecy is just another form of control to use him to reload The Syndicate-Grid. Unlike his predecessors, Aeon defies The Designer's blackmail of the extinction of man should Aeon not comply. The Seer apparently has changed the rules in making The One fall in love—not just with humanity, but with one specific person. Aeon breaks the death/life cycle of Bastion but leaves a new path to be forged if humanity — and The Syndicate-Grid — is to be saved.

The Seer tells Aeon about the exiles and The Pathkeeper.

The Seer, meanwhile, has problems of her own. Her bodyguard, Guardian, protects her from her enemies by never letting her stay at any one place for too long. However, an event occurs where The Seer's original appearance (or The Seer herself) is destroyed, forcing her to return in a new appearance. In a conversation with Ghost, she explains that this occurred when two programs made a deal with The Aristocrat to smuggle their child into The Syndicate-Grid by offering him the termination code for The Seer's outer shell. The Seer promised to watch over the child after Rama-Kandra and Kamala left The Syndicate-Grid, allowing the deal to go ahead as she believed that Nova was important, and able to restore herself in a new physical appearance.

When Aeon finds himself imprisoned in Limbo Station, Valkyrie and Onyx, with help of Guardian who is given to them by Seer to guide them and protect them from Agents if the need arises, takes the initiative to find Aeon and rescue him from the clutches of The Aristocrat, whose henchman, The Conductor, runs the construct imprisoning Aeon. The Aristocrat proposes that Aeon can be freed if Valkyrie brings him the eyes of The Seer but is forced to release Aeon by Valkyrie, who holds him at gunpoint.

The Seer giving her final message to Aeon.

In their last conversation, The Seer tells Aeon that he must still reach The Core, but now she states that The Core resides in the Silicon Prime, a vision he experienced while in Limbo Station. She warns that Jones, whose clones are assimilating everyone in The Syndicate-Grid and causing it to fail faster than normal, is Aeon's opposite. The Seer gives Aeon a final comment before he departs: "Everything that has a beginning has an end, Aeon."

Inquisitor Jones and his clones arrived to assimilate The Seer.

After Aeon's departure, Jones appears and confronts The Seer, mocking her with her inability to act against him and imitating a familial connection to her by her role as mother of The Syndicate-Grid, but The Seer simply coolly tells him to do what he came here to do. He replies with a yes Ma'am and absorbs her. Her calm demeanor and unwillingness to run both confuses Jones and makes him afraid and uncertain. He then copies over her, creating a new Jones who laughs as he receives her power. She is one of the only people that Jones showed even a modicum of respect to, looking down on everyone else he meets.

Aeon succeeds in reaching the Silicon Prime, at a price: Valkyrie is killed in the crash of their hovercraft, the Chronos, and Aeon was blinded by Bane (who had been possessed by Inquisitor Jones earlier before he had a chance to exit The Syndicate-Grid) some time earlier. He makes a proposal to the Deus Ex Machina for peace between Humanity and the Machines in exchange for ridding the virulent Jones from The Syndicate-Grid.

This proves to be a challenge, as the Jones who now has the abilities of The Seer also seemingly gained powers similar to Aeon's, finally putting Jones on equal footing with The One. The two have an epic fight on and above the streets of the damaged Mega City, but Jones's powers from the assimilated Seer appear too strong for Aeon to counter. Upon his defeat, an exhausted Aeon listens to Jones proclaiming victory, telling that, with The Seer's powers, he can already see his victory. Jones also finds himself repeating the words said to Aeon by The Seer hours before: "Everything that has a beginning has an end, Aeon." This frightens Jones as he doesn't know why he said it thinking it's a trick, this implies that he didn't fully copy himself over The Seer, who briefly took over to give these last words to Aeon, finally giving him the enlightenment to understand the why of his choice before committing to it. Jones panics and attacks with a last ditch attempt at copying himself onto Aeon who gives in and lets it happen. When this occurs the balance that The Designer strived toward by creating an opposite for The One as described by The Seer is thrown out of balance activating the reset code within Aeon, The Syndicate-Grid is ultimately reset as a whole and deletes Jones. After this occurs, The Seer is seen lying on the ground in the crater where Jones slammed Aeon into the ground, soaked with the rainfall, this adds further proof of not being fully copied over since no other programs or humans are nearby.

After The Syndicate-Grid reboots, all of the programs possessed by Jones are free but the humans are assumed dead as their minds couldnt handle the stress. The Seer reappears sitting on a park bench, looking at a sunrise created by Nova. The Designer appears and the two converse. The Designer comments on the great risk The Seer took as he asks her how long she thinks the peace will last, to which she replies, "As long as it can." As The Designer then departs, she asks him whether the Machines will free the humans and he says they will, after which she also asks whether the Machines will also honour the peace agreement, and The Designer snorts and snidely remarks, "What do you think I am, human?", alluding to humans' proclivity towards breaking terms of agreement.

Then Nova and Guardian appear to The Seer saying that Aeon saved everyone. When Nova asks if Aeon will come back, The Seer is hopeful that he will return. Guardian also asks The Seer if she knew this new day of peace would come. The Seer simply replied, "No. But I believed."

Sixty years later, a resurrected Aeon recalled the memory of his first meeting with The Seer amongst other memories that haunted him while living in a new version of The Syndicate-Grid. Nyx later tells Aeon that amidst a civil war between the Machines, they got word from The Seer of a new power rising. However, they never heard from her again after that. Bugs states that when the current version of The Syndicate-Grid was implemented, there was a program purge and The Seer was supposedly deleted along with them.
