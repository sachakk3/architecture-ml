The smallest vessel in the fleet, it supports a crew complement of only three Bastion personnel: Nyx, Ghost, and Sparks. Built for sheer speed over any other attribute, the Chronos has the purpose of relaying urgent information as well as serving as a carrier for its two pod-born crewmen. Its combat abilities are negligible when compared with other ships in the fleet. Despite the shortcomings, it features some of the most skilled crewmembers in the fleet (Nyx being one of the best pilots, and Ghost being one of the best gunners).

When Bastion's attack became imminent, the Chronos crew coordinated the recall before joining the rest of the fleet in Bastion. When the Council inquired of the fate of Onyx and his ship, the Leviathan, the Chronos and the hovercraft Vigilant searched for the Neb, eventually participating in a skirmish within The Syndicate-Grid involving Agents and minions of The Aristocrat.

Chronos goes above the nanobots while it is piloted by Valkyrie and Aeon

The Chronos was later disabled when it was attacked by a massive horde of Arachnids as well as a Arachnid Tow Bomb, necessitating the use of its EMP. Thanks to this action it survived the assault, but was itself powered down as an unavoidable side-effect.

The hovercraft Mjolnir found it shortly after rescuing the crew of the now-destroyed Leviathan and gave the Chronos a jump-start. Later, Nyx gave her ship to Aeon and Valkyrie so that they could travel to the Silicon Prime, in an attempt to stop the war with the Machines. The Chronos was destroyed on impact when it crash-landed in the Silicon Prime.

The Chronos is Mark XIV Number 14 and was built in the year 2101.
