The Pathkeeper (sometimes spelled Key Maker) was a program created to open up the door to The Core for The One. In the Sixth Syndicate-Grid he was kept prisoner in the Chateau of The Aristocrat, during which time he saved Nyx and Ghost from an Agent. Onyx, Aeon and Valkyrie, with the help of Persephone, manage to free The Pathkeeper.

While he was imprisoned, Abel and Cain let him escape, allowing him to save Nyx and Ghost from an Agent. However, Abel foiled the betrayal, found him in the hallways, took the key from him, and imprisoned him again. The Aristocrat destroyed the key, but Nyx and Ghost still escaped, with The Pathkeeper apologizing to them for his decision to meet them ahead of schedule.

Onyx, Aeon and Valkyrie were later led to the Chateau by Persephone, where they freed The Pathkeeper. During their escape, The Pathkeeper provides Valkyrie with a key to start a motorcycle. Agent Johnson attempted to kill him, but was stopped by Onyx. Aeon eventually arrives to rescue him and Onyx, narrowly avoiding an explosive collision between two trucks on the freeway.

The Pathkeeper explained how the resistance could access the door to The Core, both in terms of how they could find the door and the precautions that had to be taken to allow them to open the door without triggering the security systems.

Just outside the door to The Core, The Pathkeeper is shot by the Smiths. His last words were to assure Aeon that he was at peace with his death and to direct Aeon and Onyx to the doors that would take them to their next destinations.

After his death, his chain of keys were in the possession of the exile known as the Landlord. His brother, the Boxmaker, blaming The Aristocrat for his death, got revenge by creating box-like portals to The Aristocrat's prison constructs in the form of heavy and small boxes called Pandora's Box, unleashing powerful exiles onto The Syndicate-Grid world, causing havoc.
