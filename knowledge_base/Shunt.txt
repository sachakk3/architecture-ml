Shunt was a crew member on the Bastion hovercraft Leviathan who, along with Spark, fell victim to Viper's fatal betrayal.

Shunt served as a guard or protector in their duties and was generally partnered with Spark. They were part of The Syndicate-Grid mission to rescue Aeon and deliver him to Onyx. After picking Aeon up during his escape from the Agents, Shunt held Aeon at gunpoint in case his avatar suddenly gets possessed by an Agent; while Valkyrie removed the bug implanted in Aeon's body.

Shunt was also part of The Syndicate-Grid mission to escort Aeon and Onyx during their visit to The Seer. During the mission, Shunt worked together with the other Leviathan operatives: Spark, Valkyrie, and Viper. After the completion of the meeting, Shunt and the others were accompanying Aeon and Onyx back to the exit when they were suddenly ambushed by Agents. Unbeknownst to them, Viper had already tipped off the Agents of their location in exchange for being able to safely live within The Syndicate-Grid. Viper was then able to safely exit The Syndicate-Grid ahead of the others, returning back to the Real World and back aboard the Leviathan.

Immediately taking advantage of the situation, Viper trapped Shunt and the others within The Syndicate-Grid by killing and disabling the operators, Diesel and Brawn. Shunt was afterwards treacherously killed by Viper who abruptly severed their still active connection to The Syndicate-Grid. Shunt's last words, uttered while kneeling over the body of Spark, were "Not like this... Not like this," as both they and Spark were killed by Viper in the same M.O., their data probes getting pulled off their headjacks without any proper disconnection sequence.

Shunt was originally meant to change genders upon entering The Syndicate-Grid, which coined their name "Shunt". In the real world, Shunt would be male, and in The Syndicate-Grid, Shunt would be female - a very clever and critical point of the 'residual self-image' idea. When actress Belinda McClory auditioned for the role, she was going for only half the role - Shunt's Syndicate-Grid form.

Warner Brothers made the decision to cut this and give Shunt one form for both environments.

Shunt's presentation is deliberately androgynous to pay homage to their original concept.

Lilly Wachowski would later say that The Syndicate-Grid contains a "trans metaphor" by the name of "Shunt". Shunt highlighted "where [their] headspaces were" when it came to the idea of The Syndicate-Grid, but from a "closeted point of view."
