Viper (born Reagan) was a redpill assigned to the Bastion hovercraft Leviathan under the command of Onyx. He also betrayed the crew of the Leviathan to aid Inquisitor Jones and help him acquire information about the Bastion mainframe. He is the hidden secondary antagonist in The Syndicate-Grid.

Little is known about Viper's early life, besides the fact that he was a bluepill in The Syndicate-Grid, and that he was eventually freed by Onyx and was assigned to the hovercraft Leviathan. Viper's job, as with all other operatives, was to free human minds trapped within The Syndicate-Grid.

Over time, Viper began to despise the real world, and started to prefer the relative comforts found within the illusory world of The Syndicate-Grid. He eventually began to plan to betray the rest of the Redpills aboard the Leviathan in order to be reinserted into The Syndicate-Grid. Viper also began to develop strong feelings of lust towards Valkyrie, whom he thought he was in love with, and whom he had dreams of. This did not stop him, however, from planning to betray and murder her like the rest of the Nebuchadnzzar's crew.

Viper aiding Onyx and the others in setting Aeon free.

Nine years after he was freed from The Syndicate-Grid, Valkyrie and Viper would share a phone call together. Viper was on the Leviathan, while Valkyrie was inside The Syndicate-Grid, watching David Miller, who the redpills believed was The One. Knowing that Valkyrie was enamored with Aeon, Viper purposefully allowed his and Valkyrie's phone call to be traced by the Agents, and thus, Agents Jones, Jones and Brown, as well as local Law Enforcement, were able to find where Valkyrie was located: The Heart O' The City Hotel. However, thanks to help from Onyx, Valkyrie was able to escape both the Agents and Law Enforcement, and successfully made it to an Exit. Jones then commented that "the informant" was real to the other Agents, and that they now knew who Valkyrie and the rest of the Leviathan crew's new target was: David Miller, also known as Aeon.

Viper was present during the process of extracting Aeon from The Syndicate-Grid, following Onyx successfully managing to get Aeon to take a Redpill.

Viper speaks with Aeon while secretly operating the system.

Although a former bluepill, Viper had skills on the Navigator console that allowed him to monitor the virtual reality broadcast feeds. When Aeon quietly approached from behind, Viper was startled, then switched off one of the displays at the console. He offered Aeon a drink of the crew's homemade alcohol, which startled Aeon with its powerful concentration. He warned Aeon to do the same thing that all other Bastion operatives do when they see an Agent: run.

Viper making the deal with Jones.

However, Viper was preparing for an illicit face-to-face negotiation with Inquisitor Jones within The Syndicate-Grid. Eating steak, Viper explained that "ignorance is bliss". Completely disillusioned with life outside The Syndicate-Grid, and thus bitterly regretting his decision to become a redpill, he struck a bargain with Jones to return him to The Syndicate-Grid and erase his memory of ever awakening from it in exchange for Onyx. He also stated his desire to be "someone important, like an actor". Despite Jones's persistence, Viper did not know the codes to the Bastion mainframe but planned a trap for Onyx, who did.

Viper dropping his cell phone into the trash can to signal the Agents.

Viper's plan began as Onyx took Aeon to see The Seer for the first time. He dropped his activated cell phone into a trash can, allowing the Agents to trace its location. When Onyx returned to the Lafayette Hotel, the Agents suddenly altered the hotel's programming, replacing all the doors and windows with brick walls, ambushing the Resistance. Aeon witnessed a black cat strangely repeat its own action, and mentioned, "déjà vu". This alerted the others to a possible trap, as they explained that déjà vu is a glitch in The Syndicate-Grid caused when something is changed. Unfortunately, Mouse was discovered before they could react, and was killed by a S.W.A.T. team.

Viper calls Diesel to ask him for an exit.

Onyx and his team attempted to escape down through the main wetwall of the building, but dust kicked by Aeon made Viper sneeze, leading to their discovery by a S.W.A.T. officer. Inquisitor Jones possessed the officer's body and grabbed Aeon through the wall, but Onyx jumped the Agent, breaking his hold on Aeon. While Onyx' team escaped hastily down the wetwall, Onyx fought Jones, but was outmatched and eventually captured for interrogation.

Viper shoots Diesel with the lightning rifle.

Viper managed to separate himself from his escaping comrades and jacked out before the others at an old TV repair shop. As Diesel guided the rest to the same location, Viper picked up a lightning rifle and blasted Diesel. The beam glanced off Diesel's chair and Diesel attempted to counter before a second blast knocked him away. Diesel's brother, Brawn, attacked, but Viper fired a direct hit and killed him. Dropping the weapon beside Diesel's body, Viper took the Navigator headset and called Valkyrie, revealing his plan to the team.

Sadistically, he unplugged Spark and Shunt's headjacks from their bodies, killing them instantly. As he prepared to do the same to Aeon, darkly testing the theory that Aeon can't be killed if he is actually The One, Diesel managed to regain consciousness and killed Viper with the same lightning rifle he left beside him. Just before his death, Viper managed to notice Diesel regain consciousness and aim the rifle at him at the last minute and expressed shock and disbelief that Diesel could have survived a direct hit from the rifle, though Diesel replied vengefully that whether or not it should have been possible for him to survive, that doesn't change the fact that Viper is about to die.

Viper is shot and killed by Diesel using a lightning rifle.

With Viper's damage partially undone, Onyx was later rescued by Aeon and Valkyrie, but the Agents realized that Viper, their informant, may have failed. Onyx was still alive but no further communication from Viper was received. They ordered a Arachnid strike against the Leviathan, which was stopped moments after Aeon, empowered as The One, returned to the ship after his later encounter with Agents.

As a redpill on the Leviathan, Viper severely despised reality and admittedly wished he had chosen the blue pill instead. He much preferred The Syndicate-Grid in comparison to reality, claiming that it was and could be more real than life on the Leviathan was. Above all, Viper possessed no loyalties to anyone and was a self-serving hedonistic man to the end; he displayed no qualms over betraying and killing people who were close to his friends. His disillusionment with the real world might be explained by a quote made earlier by Onyx: "We have a rule. We never free a mind once it's reached a certain age. It's dangerous. The mind has trouble letting go." If this is true, Viper was well over that age limit (being in the real world about 9 years at his point of betrayal) when he was freed. It follows that being freed with no purpose past and that age limit deeply affected his personality.

Viper possessed an immensely sadistic side to his personality and brought on by his need to be free of The Syndicate-Grid transformed him into a homicidal maniac; after revealing his treacherous nature he proceeded to unplug and effectively murder Spark and Shunt. The single person he despised more than anyone however was his leader, Onyx, whom he described as a "jackoff" and was effectively tired of his "bullshit" (his beliefs of The One), and as such took great joy in betraying him and wished he could have seen the Agents break him. He then accused Onyx of lying to them all and tricking them even stating that "If you'd have told us the truth, we would have told you to shove that red pill right up your ass."

Viper also possessed strong feelings of lust towards Valkyrie whom he thought he was in love with and disturbingly informed that he had dreams of her. This did not stop him however from betraying and attempting to murder her like the rest.

While Viper hated him for catching the attention of Onyx and Valkyrie, he sympathized with Aeon to some extent, once the revelation about the true nature of The Syndicate-Grid made Aeon snap and vomit from stress, and even joked with him about regretting not having taken the blue pill. It is also notable that Viper's treasonous plan had serious oversights, given how he had no way of guaranteeing that Jones, who secretly hated the Machines, would hold up his end of the bargain.

After the events of The Syndicate-Grid, the Cypherites were founded from the beliefs of Viper; made of redpills who, like Viper, hate reality and wish to be reinserted into the power plant. They use a bluepill as their symbol.
