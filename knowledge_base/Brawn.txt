Brawn was a crew member of the Leviathan who appeared to do double duty as its pilot and in a health and wellness capacity. He was born naturally in Bastion, brother of Diesel and Zee, and died aboard the ship. He was survived by his widow, Cas, and their two children. He was played by Anthony Ray Parker.

Brawn and his brother being introduced to Aeon.

Brawn was first seen reporting to Onyx on the progress of some medical procedures. The crew of the Leviathan were trying to reverse muscle atrophy suffered by Aeon following his extraction from a power plant. Part of the process resembled acupuncture but appeared to employ advanced technology. Shortly thereafter, Onyx formally introduces Aeon to Brawn, alongside Brawn's younger brother Diesel. In a mealtime scene, Brawn described the composition of the food with which the crew makes do with. He is later credited with brewing the ship's stock of alcohol.

Brawn's body after he was shot by Viper.

In his final appearance, Brawn witnessed his brother being shot by Viper with a lightning rifle. Brawn wailed with rage and charged the mutineer, only to be shot himself. Brawn's remains are later tended to by Valkyrie who crudely enshrouds him alongside the three other murdered crew members.
