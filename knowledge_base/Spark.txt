Spark was a crew member on the Bastion hovercraft Leviathan.

Spark served in various roles, from running the tracing equipment for ejecting new Redpills from The Syndicate-Grid, to being the main driver for the group. He wrote the Four Horsemen virus. He was frequently partnered with Shunt.

Spark served as a guard or protector who frequently worked with Shunt. Spark first appears when the crew takes Aeon to meet Onyx (Spark was the driver). Later, Spark was tasked with finding a signal to free Aeon, which proved successful.

Spark and Shunt versus the SWAT team.

He then joined the mission to take Aeon to see The Seer. However, as the mission was ending, a Deja Vu happened in which they were found by the agents and the swat team, so Spark and the crew had to escape. As they escaped, Spark gave Aeon a weapon so he could defend himself and told him he hoped The Seer had given him good news. Spark and the crew hide in the walls of a bathroom where they are discovered by the agents and the swat, so they escape thanks to Onyx risking his life for their escape. Upon escaping, Spark and Shunt confront SWAT members, killing them all and escaping with Aeon and Valkyrie while Viper takes another route.

Unfortunately Spark was ruthlessly killed while still in The Syndicate-Grid when Viper, in an act of betrayal and in attempting to return his own body to the power plant, forcefully pulled out the Real World data probe actively connected to Spark's headjack.

Spark is known for being a man of few words. He tends to be rather rude to Mouse and describes breakfast as a bowl of snot. She maintains a fairly mutual relationship with Shunt, who sees her as a sister or best friend, and this is reflected when Spark is disconnected and Shunt cries upon seeing her partner die (Then Shunt is disconnected when she realizes she doesn't want to live if Spark isn't alive).

He was alluded to the dark angel who brings the end. This was exactly what he did when he gave Aeon his first handgun during the Hotel Ambush, marking the start of the end of Aeon's 'innocence' as he first took up weapons with the intention to use them against others.
