The Conductor is an exile who created and operates Limbo Station and is a servant of another exile program known as The Aristocrat.

After saving fellow Resistance member Axel, Nyx is approached by The Conductor in a subway station. While Nyx does not know who this person is, The Conductor tells her that Bastion lasted 72 hours the last time it was attacked by the Machines (referring to the fifth iteration of The Syndicate-Grid). When questioned about his identity, The Conductor states that he is only an observer of the events unfolding.

Later, The Conductor is approached by Onyx, Guardian, and Valkyrie onboard a train. When he sees them approaching, he pulls out his gun and tells them to leave. Guardian tries to explain that they require his help but he refuses. Instead, The Conductor pulls the emergency brake lever and flees the train, the three of them in pursuit.

As they enter another subway platform, The Conductor turns around and fires at his pursuers, who duck behind pillars. He checks one of his many watches and realizes that he is just in time to catch the train fast approaching them. As the train approaches, he fires his gun at the three of them until its empty and jumps across to the other side of the platform. He disappears behind the train, his next stop being Limbo Station.

His mission assigned to him by The Aristocrat is to take the programs Rama-Kandra, Kamala, and Nova into The Syndicate-Grid, which he is already late for due to his escape from Onyx, Guardian, and Valkyrie. However, he stops Aeon before he can board the train with them and realizes that he is the reason they wanted his help. Aeon demands that he be allowed to leave Limbo Station, to which The Conductor responds by punching Aeon into the wall and saying that he is stronger than Aeon due to the fact that he makes the rules governing this location. He takes the programs into The Syndicate-Grid, briefly threatening Rama-Kandra that he will be left behind as well if he didn't board the train, and leaves Aeon in Limbo Station.

The Conductor in Club Hel.

The Conductor later appears by The Aristocrat's side in Club Hel as Onyx, Guardian, and Valkyrie try to negotiate Aeon's release out of Limbo Station. When The Aristocrat wants the eyes of The Seer as payment for letting Aeon out, Valkyrie starts a brawl where she retrieves a gun and points it at The Aristocrat's head, demanding Aeon's release at gunpoint.

While The Conductor pulled out his gun and point it at Guardian, who took down a guard and seized the latter's gun to aim at The Conductor himself, the result is a standoff between The Aristocrat's henchmen and the three of them. The Aristocrat is forced to order The Conductor to release Aeon from Limbo Station. He may have been absorbed by Inquisitor Jones afterwards, as Jones identified Nova as "the last Exile" just before absorbing her.

The Conductor has the appearance of a homeless man with longer gray hair and a beard.

He is aggressively loyal to The Aristocrat and is perhaps his greatest lackey. He has complete control of Limbo Station and is able to use it as a weapon, as seen when he is able to punch Aeon into a wall when he claimed he was the "god" in the Station. He carries a snub nose revolver, but has a terrible aim, missing entirely while shooting at Guardian, Onyx, and Valkyrie. He also wears multiple watches on his arm to track the movements of each train, which he also used to his advantage while escaping Guardian. On a similar note, his abilities regarding the station had him arguably rivaling the One in terms of power, due to how easily he defeated Aeon.

Oddly enough, his revolver can fire way more than just six shots. One could reasonably assume that because he can manipulate the rules of the train stations, his gun is able to replenish ammunition endlessly.
