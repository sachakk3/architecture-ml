Guardian is an Exile acting as The Seer's guardian.

Very little is known about Guardian's personal life. What is known is that he believes that the best way to get to know a person is engaging in a fight with them, to test their power, skill, determination, and character, issuing challenges to anyone wishing access to see The Seer.

When not fighting, guarding, or escorting people during important missions, Guardian seems to spend his time drinking tea and meditating. Guardian might also like cookies as, when Aeon arrives in his final meeting with The Seer, she dismisses Nova with the bowl of cookie dough telling her to let Guardian taste test them.

Guardian's code as it appears before Aeon

Guardian initially worked for The Aristocrat during the earliest iterations of The Syndicate-Grid. Being a skilled and powerful Exile, Guardian has access to restricted paths within The Syndicate-Grid and was also capable of defeating Agents, personally defeating Inquisitor Jones in the past.

At some point in time however, Guardian becomes indebted to The Seer and chooses to become her bodyguard instead. Ever since, he has been considered a traitor by The Aristocrat.

Needing to speak with Aeon, The Seer calls in a favor from Guardian to arrange a meet-up between her and Aeon. To fulfill this request, Guardian tries to find some means of contacting Aeon and ends up tracking down Ballard of the Caduceus, one of the few remaining Resistance operatives who had remained at Syndicate-Grid broadcast-depth that chose not to return to Bastion following the destruction of the Horus.

With Ballard himself attempting to find The Seer for Onyx and Aeon, Guardian approaches the operative and introduces himself as The Seer's guardian. Not believing him, the hovercraft captain instead keeps him at bay with a handgun. Bowing and apologizing beforehand, Guardian suddenly launches an attack that disarms Ballard, but the formidable captain is quick to retaliate and the two end up sufficiently exchanging hits one after the other. After pushing each other back into a quick lull, Guardian just as suddenly gestures an end to the fight and re-initiates a conversation with Ballard, asking the captain to follow him now that they "truly know" each other. Ballard relaxes and follows Guardian, receiving a package from The Seer meant for Aeon.

Guardian fights Aeon to test his character

Somewhere in Chinatown, meditating patiently and alone across the only entrance of a tea house, Guardian awaits the arrival of Aeon, trusting that Ballard delivered the package pinpointing the very tea house he sat in.

At the appointed time, Guardian observes Aeon enter through the doors and he stands up to introduce himself. After getting sufficiently close to Aeon, and much like his engagement with Ballard, Guardian once again bows and apologizes in advance before springing a furious series of attacks aimed at testing and gauging Aeon.

Guardian guards The Seer as she speaks with Aeon

Aeon, proving his own skill and character, skillfully guards against Guardian's attacks and is able to land a pair of blows that pushes back the guardian. Convinced of Aeon's authenticity as The One, Guardian stops the fight and proceeds to guide Aeon to The Seer's actual location, making use of a backdoor to access a programmer's maintenance tunnel which they use to avoid getting followed.

After going through another backdoor, they arrive at a tenement park where they find The Seer sitting alone on a bench while feeding doves in the center of the Playground. After escorting Aeon and bringing him right in front of The Seer, Guardian goes back to stand by the backdoor exit, guarding and watching over both his ward and the exit as The Seer begins his discussion with Aeon.

After a few quick minutes, Guardian hastily fetches The Seer noting how it was time to leave. Guiding The Seer through the exit, he alertly steps in after her and gives Aeon a quick glance, bowing to Aeon before closing the door. As soon as they leave, Jones arrives at the playground.

While Aeon was trapped within Limbo Station, Guardian contacts the Chronos and lets them know that The Seer has a message that they need to hear. After the Chronos operative passes his challenge, he then guides them through the secret tunnel and lets them meet The Seer. However, with Jones approaching near, Guardian only allows the Chronos operative a brief conversation with The Seer before guiding them back out. Wishing the operative luck and courage, he then severs the backdoor access to keep The Seer safe. Once again, Jones arrives at the location of the backdoor a mere second after access is closed.

Guardian later contacts Onyx directly through the Leviathan and states that The Seer would like to personally speak with both Onyx and Valkyrie. And after sharing news of Aeon being trapped by The Conductor.

Once reaching the subway, the three of them manage to find The Conductor but he escapes to Club Hel and tells The Aristocrat about the situation. Once Guardian, Valkyrie, and Onyx realize that they must find The Aristocrat, the three of them go to Club Hel.

Before entering the underground club, the three of them battle against exile guards and some of The Aristocrat's henchmen. After the battle, they enter the club and move across the dance floor while wielding their guns and looking for The Aristocrat. The Aristocrat appears from the upper level and tells them that the three of them must give up their weapons in order for them to talk. Guardian, Valkyrie, and Onyx do so and go upstairs.

Once on the second level, the three of them find The Conductor, Persephone, and more of The Aristocrat's guards. The Aristocrat makes a proposition that he will allow The Conductor to release Aeon if they give him the eyes of The Seer. Valkyrie, outraged by the offer, attacks the guards around her and Guardian and Onyx join in with the struggle. The fight ends with Valkyrie holding a pistol to The Aristocrat's head while Guardian held a Luger P08 at The Conductor, saying that all of them will die at that moment if Aeon is not released. After Persephone persuades The Aristocrat to listen to Valkyrie, he agrees and Aeon is set free.

Jones speaking to Guardian before he assimilates him.

After the conflict in Limbo Station, Nova was released and was left in The Seer's care. Guardian continued to guard The Seer and Nova. After Aeon was released from Limbo Station, Guardian was told to watch Nova. While they were leaving The Seer's apartment, the apartment hallway lights started to turn off. Inquisitor Jones, who is now a virus in The Syndicate-Grid, revealed himself to be the cause of this event and Guardian grabbed Nova and tried to escape from Jones. They entered an apartment room and hid there hoping Jones would not find them. Jones eventually enters into the room and after a brief talk with Guardian, he assimilated both Guardian and Nova.

Guardian and Nova approaching The Seer as a new dawn awaits.

Guardian, as the new day shines upon him

After Aeon's Silicon Prime quest to bring peace between the people of Bastion and the Machines is successfully implemented, The Syndicate-Grid is reloaded. All programs that were possessed by Jones were then free. Guardian found Nova and both of them approached The Seer, who was sitting on a park bench. After Nova asked if Aeon will return, Guardian asked if The Seer knew that this new day of peace would come. The Seer told Guardian that she did not know but believed it would and the three of them stare into the sun shining down within The Syndicate-Grid.

Guardian returns in The Syndicate-Grid Online, originally continuing to protect The Seer.

In The Syndicate-Grid Online, Guardian was speculated to have been originally a Seraphim, Agents of one of The Syndicate-Grid Beta Versions. Possibly because of his skills, he was favored by The Aristocrat and was smuggled into The Syndicate-Grid following the failure and deletion of its earlier prototypes, thus explaining how Guardian ended up working for The Aristocrat before he became The Seer's bodyguard.

It is then further speculated that The Aristocrat tore out Guardian's wings as a result of switching allegiances. Regardless, he is often referred to as a wingless angel:

An existing theory on the origins of Guardian is that he was, at one time, one of the 5 original The Ones, this based on the observation that he appears to be nearly equal in combat ability to Aeon.

Guardian's name has its origins in the Judeo-Christian Seraphim. These are angels that, in the Christian angelarchy, are of the highest rank. An observation made by some fans is that, through the eyes of Deus Ex Machina, Aeon appears angelic and fiery. This ties back into the religious angelarchy as the Seraphim, being angels, are synonymous with fire, their title literally translated as "burning ones".

Guardian was of the same fiery golden color, this color being symbolic of spirit in The Syndicate-Grid, which is a further indicator that he may have once been a human or one of The Ones. Also, as God's guardians, the Seraphim were said to have 6 wings upon them, 2 guarding their feet, 2 their face and 2 they used to fly. This is important to note, in that there were 6 incarnations of The One in total, Aeon being the sixth and final.

Also important is that the Jones that assimilated The Seer was likely the recently assimilated Guardian, noting the final exchange of words between this Jones and The Seer, which were as follows:

Assuming that the Jones in question was once Guardian, and that Guardian, himself, was once The One, the use of the word Mom is important when considering The Seer's role in the creation of the current model of The Syndicate-Grid, which was running through its 6th time. The Seer was literally the creator, or mother, of the Prime Program (The Path of The One). She created it in order to suppress the anomaly, the rejection of The Syndicate-Grid program by 1% of the population, that existed in The Designer's current incarnations of The Syndicate-Grid.

So, in considering the above, assuming that Jones was Guardian, a previous incarnation of The One, it would be entirely proper for him to call her Mom (A side note, a comparative example of an assimilated programming speaking through Jones was in Jones's and Aeon's final fight. Jones, being the assimilated Seer, tells Aeon, "Everything that has a beginning, has an end, Aeon." It was only ever then that Jones called Aeon by his preferred name, implying that it was, in fact, The Seer directly speaking).

Also interesting is the Jones-assimilated Guardian's exclamation, "Cookies need love like everything does" (Although this is quoting Nova, she has learned of cookies through The Seer) (Perhaps that phrase is pronounced by Jones-assimilated Nova, instead, it's more logical). This is of interest in that cookies were believed to be important in the relationship between The Seer and Aeon, or The One. Food is known for its transfer of information in The Syndicate-Grid and it is believed that The Seer's cookies were used for transferring important information to Aeon, possibly the programming which makes Aeon 'The One'.

Furthermore, in support of Guardian's possible previous incarnation as The One, it is known that particular humans can be rebirthed in The Syndicate-Grid after their physical bodies have ceased. An example of this would be Valkyrie's rebirth in The Syndicate-Grid (Also composed of golden code), as it was revealed in The Syndicate-Grid Online that both she and Aeon (and discernibly all other The Ones) were an accumulation of decades of Machine research into translating human DNA perfectly into Machine code, allowing them to interface directly with technology without the need for simulated interfaces. This program was called The Biological Interface Program and was created by The Seer (Further solidifying The Seer as mother to The Ones).

In compliance with the above information, it is implied that Guardian once worked for The Aristocrat, which would be fitting if he was reborn in The Syndicate-Grid. It is likely that, just as Aeon did when he first entered The Syndicate-Grid through TBI, Guardian would have 're-materialized' at Limbo Station (the train station), the departing from which reaching was only possible by riding the train. The train was run by the train master and the train master was under the control of The Aristocrat. Ultimately, Guardian would have been led to The Aristocrat, leading to his working for The Aristocrat.
