The smiling Diesel, standing next to his brother, is first introduced to Aeon (and the audience) by Onyx during a brief tour of the Leviathan. Diesel assists with prepping Aeon and jacking him into the Construct for a full briefing on the state of the world.

Diesel shows great zeal and enthusiasm concerning life in Bastion and the legends surrounding the One. The day after Aeon fully regains consciousness following his extraction, Diesel is given charge of his basic training using neural interactive software. He also shows something of a rebellious streak, postponing "operational programs" he claims are "boring", and starting with martial arts lessons instead. He then marvels at Aeon's stamina as the recruit learns ten hours' worth of simulated, hand-to-hand combat styles with no breaks.

Diesel showing Aeon a jiu-jitsu imprint program.

After Onyx takes over the training process, Diesel continues to oversee the goings-on in the Sparring program, Jump program, and the Agent training program, loading and pausing them on command. He works despite a distraction when Mouse touches a display after leading in spectators. Diesel appears to be trying not to get his hopes up about the possibility that Aeon is The One, saying that "no one ever makes the first jump."

When Viper stages his one-man mutiny, he attacks Diesel with a Lightning rifle, seemingly wounding him and killing his brother, Brawn, and then proceeds to kill fellow crew-members Spark and Shunt by disconnecting them from The Syndicate-Grid.

Diesel, after recovering, prepares to shoot Viper.

Just when the assailant is doubting that "some kind of a miracle" will thwart his gambit, Diesel recovers enough to kill him with the lightning rifle, which Viper carelessly discarded near Diesel's body, although not before vengefully replying to Viper's disbelief at his survival that, whether or not it should have been possible, Viper is still going to die. Diesel jacked out Aeon and Valkyrie and discussed about their plans for the captured Onyx.

Diesel monitors on Aeon and Valkyrie during the rescue mission.

With over half of the crew dead and Onyx captured by Agents, Diesel makes the difficult decision to sacrifice him by disconnection. He makes a funereal apology, saying the great captain was "like a father" to him, and prepares to remove the plug from his head. Aeon stops him and urges that a rescue mission be attempted instead.

Diesel ultimately agrees and performs his usual duties in assisting as Aeon and Valkyrie attempt to rescue Onyx. He supplies "lots of guns", as required by Aeon, and assists them in locating an Exit after Onyx is freed from captivity. He then watches in awe as Aeon's death reverses and finds his faith in The One validated.

It is unclear if Diesel's death was the result of the severe wound he sustained from the energy bolt, or of unrelated causes. Regardless, he is deceased by the time of The Syndicate-Grid Reloaded, with his place as Navigator filled by his brother-in-law, Link, who is married to Diesel's sister Zee.

Diesel's actor, Marcus Chong, reportedly demanded to be paid more for appearing in the sequels; a breakdown in talks between him and the Wachowskis ensued, leading Diesel to be written out and replaced by Link.
